About Massachusetts Farm to School

Local food, real results,
across the Commonwealth.

We connect Massachusetts farms and fisheries to school cafeterias and classrooms, building healthier communities one meal at a time.

What is farm to school?

Farm to school brings local food onto school trays, hands-on agriculture into classrooms, and gardens onto school grounds.

It is three connected practices working together. Each one helps Massachusetts students eat better, learn where their food comes from, and keeps food dollars in the local economy.

01

Local procurement

Schools buy fresh food directly from Massachusetts farms and fisheries.

02

Food & nutrition education

Students learn about food and agriculture through real, hands-on lessons.

03

School gardens

Growing spaces turn school grounds into living classrooms.

$3M+ Local-food dollars moved Direct spending channeled from school budgets to Massachusetts farms and fisheries.

10K+ Students reached Children eating local food and learning where it comes from, in cafeterias and classrooms.

Mission & Vision

Our mission

Strengthening local farms and fisheries and promoting healthy communities through schools.

Our vision

A Massachusetts where every student eats local, learns where their food comes from, and grows up connected to the land and waters that feed them.

Our story.

It started in 2004 with a simple question: why was so little of the food on Massachusetts school trays grown in Massachusetts?

A small group of farmers, educators, and school nutrition directors set out to close that gap, one district and one grower at a time. Twenty years on, that work has grown into a statewide network running Harvest of the Month and administering the MA FRESH grant program.

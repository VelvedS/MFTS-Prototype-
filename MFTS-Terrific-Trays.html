Terrific Trays

Vote on the best local school lunch tray in Massachusetts.

What it is

An ongoing competition celebrating Massachusetts school nutrition programs that put local food on the tray. Schools across the Commonwealth submit photographs of trays featuring local ingredients. Each month a Terrific Tray is highlighted and at the end of the school year, the public votes on a Terrific Tray Winner.

Why it matters

The trays you'll see here aren't beautiful by accident. Behind each one is a school nutrition director who created the menu, a farmer who grew the food, and a kitchen team that scratch-cooked it. We highlight that work because it deserves to be highlighted — and because seeing it makes it easier for other districts to follow.

Partnership & prize

Terrific Trays is run in partnership with the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education's Office for Food and Nutrition Programs (FNP). Terrific Tray winners will receive a check for $2,500 to reinvest in their School Nutrition Program thanks to the generosity of the Henry P. Kendall Foundation.

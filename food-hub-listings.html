Eastern MA

Boston Food Hub

91 Martin St., Acton, MA, 01720  https://www.bostonfoodhub.org/

Boston Food Hub is a wholesale aggregator and distributor of fresh produce, dedicated to supporting local and regional growers by streamlining the process of bringing farmers’ most plentiful harvests to the wholesale market.

ProductsVegetables, Fruit
DeliveryBoston Food Hub has a $250 delivery minimum and can deliver to schools throughout Eastern and Central Massachusetts.

Annie Broad, Associate Food Hub Director | (781) 546-4700 | sales@bostonfoodhub.org | https://www.bostonfoodhub.org/

Regional Partner

Food Connects

22 Browne Ct Unit 110, Brattleboro, VT 05301  shop.foodconnects.org

Food Connects operates a food hub that delivers local and regionally sourced products from over 120 farms and producers to over 100 schools throughout VT, NH, and western MA. Our mission is to cultivate healthy food and farm connections in cafeterias, classrooms and communities.

ProductsVegetables, Fruit, Meat, Poultry, Beans/Grains, Dairy, Seafood, Eggs, Maple Syrup, Honey, Value Added Products
DeliveryFood Connects is thrilled to share that we have launched a new delivery route with the primary agenda to serve more schools! We take I-91 South from Bernardston as far as Holyoke. We can deliver to your district every Monday. Your minimum is based on school location(s).

Tom Brewton, Institutional Sales Associate | 802-258-1546 | tom@foodconnects.org

Eastern MA

New Entry Food Hub

733 Cabot Street, Beverly, MA 01915  https://nesfp.nutrition.tufts.edu/foodhub

The New Entry Food Hub focuses on supporting small-scale beginning farmers to establish and grow their businesses through training, technical assistance and marketing support. Your wholesale purchases from New Entry allow us to continue connecting beginning farmers to institutional markets and supports our mission of bringing fresh and nutritious produce to underserved communities in the Greater Boston Area.

ProductsVegetables, Fruit, Value Added Products
DeliveryOffered year-round and free of charge for orders of $200 or more. We currently deliver Tuesday-Thursday throughout the Greater Boston Area and the Northshore. We are willing to expand our delivery range to bring local produce to schools.

Sara Poggi Davis, New Entry Food Hub Program Manager | 978-656-1654 | foodhub@nesfp.org

Regional Partner

Red Tomato

10 Sims Ave, Suite 102, Providence, RI 02909  redtomato.org

Red Tomato is a non-profit food hub working with ~40 growers in the Northeast region. EcoCertified is our signature program, which is an ecological growing certification for apple, peach and stone fruit farmers in the Northeast. We are currently working with Whitsons to deliver EcoCertified local/regional apples and assorted vegetables to schools in MA & CT.

ProductsVegetables, Fruit
DeliveryDetails are dependent on location, number of deliveries required, etc. Red Tomato develops customer-specific programs, so please reach out with your needs!

Alessandra Cancalosi , Trade & Marketing Associate | 262-527-5550 | acancalosi@redtomato.org

Western MA

Western MA Food Processing Center

324 Wells St, Greenfield, MA 01301  wmfpc.org

The Western Mass Food Processing Center offers value added co-manufacturing, co-packaging, as well as ambient, refrigerated and frozen storage and cross docking, with over 80 pallet spaces per section.

ProductsVegetables, Fruit, Cross Docking, Storage
ShippingWMFPC staff receive/prepares client orders for incoming/ outgoing shipments.

Toni Hall | 413-774-7204 ex 116 | Tonih@fccdc.org | Food Entrepreneurship Program Coordinator

Western MA

Marty's Local

10 Greenfield Road / PO Box 276, South Deerfield, MA 01373  www.martyslocal.com

Marty’s Local strengthens our regional food system by linking exceptional farmers and food producers with local institutions. We are a wholesale distributor of local, high-quality fruits, vegetables, flour & grains, dairy, meats, cheeses, and specialty foods, along with produce from farther afield.

ProductsVegetables, Fruit, Meat, Poultry, Beans/Grains, Dairy, Eggs, Maple Syrup, Honey, Value Added Products
Delivery$350 minimum. Delivery throughout western Massachusetts from the Berkshires east through the Pioneer Valley, Barre, Worcester to Metro West. Deliveries to eastern Massachusetts may be possible.

Sandra Thomas, Director of Marketing & Customer Support | 413.347.9401 | sandra@martyslocal.com

Central MA

World Farmers

769 Main Street, Lancaster, MA 01523  www.worldfarmers.org

World Farmers advocates for and supports small-scale immigrant, refugee, and historically underserved farmers from farm to market. Our aggregation and distribution work follows a model similar to a growers association and a food hub and provides a stable market for immigrant and refugee farmers within our network. We support all vegetable farmers who specialize in cultural crop production.

ProductsVegetables, Beans/Grains, Value Added Products
DeliverySpecific range and minimums for delivery will be reviewed and discussed according to each account.

Jessy Gill, Program & Policy Director | 978-706-7935 | sales@worldfarmers.org

LFW
Central MA

Local Food Works

325 Lindell Ave., Leominster, MA 01453  www.growingplaces.org

Local Food Works is a Regional Food Center that collectively aggregates and distributes local food products from 30+ farms in North Central MA. We offer wholesale local produce and are able to connect you with local farms in our region that sell eggs, dairy products and meat. Very soon we will be opening a food processing facility in Gardner, MA with the ability to lightly process local fruits and vegetables (cubed butternut squash, frozen broccoli florets) to make preparation easier for institutions like schools.

ProductsVegetables, Fruit, Value Added Products
DeliveryDelivery to North Central MA is part of their mission to create an equitable and sustainable local food system.

Ayn Yeagle, Executive Director – Growing Places – Local Food Works Fiscal Sponsor | 508-331-6663 | ayn@growingplaces.org

Eastern MA

Coastal Foodshed

127 Rodney French Blvd, STE 312, New Bedford MA 027444  coastalfoodshed.org

Coastal Foodshed is a farmer food hub with a mission to make it easier for farmers to sell local food and for the community to purchase it. We aggregate and distribute local food from over 65 farmers and producers through D2C and B2B channels.

We have a $300 delivery minimum and deliver to Southeastern MA.

Stephanie Perks, Executive Director | 508-259-2647 | stephanie@coastalfoodshed.org

Terrific Trays · School Nutrition Programs

Submit Your Tray

Want to submit a Terrific Tray? Read below for submission guidelines, scoring criteria and where to find support with your entry.

Submit Your Tray

Submission Guidelines

All five components that make up a reimbursable meal (fruit, vegetable, meat/meat alternate, grain, and fluid milk) should be visible in the submission photo for consideration. Submissions must be from a school meal program operating the National School Lunch Program in Massachusetts. The submission must demonstrate a tray that will be menued and served to students in your school meal program during the applicable school year.

Additionally, preference will be given to photographs that:

  • Feature specifically Massachusetts grown, raised, or caught foods with preference given to trays that feature at least 2 or more local ingredients that represent different meal components
  • Do not contain foods that are wrapped in plastic
  • Have a shareable recipe that corresponds with the photograph, with preference given to recipes that are standardized for use in School Nutrition Program
  • Demonstrate an innovative menu item, with scratch made elements, that is culturally-relevant or represents student voice
  • Showcase unflavored milk or a nutritionally equivalent milk alternative (Lactaid)
  • Have a strong visual composition (a high resolution photograph representing a visually appealing, plated meal with a diversity of colors, clear focus, and appropriate portioning for the National School Lunch Program) and do not contain foods that are wrapped in plastic
  • Are submitted by districts that represent geographic diversity among current Terrific Tray winners
01

To ensure we are celebrating a diversity of trays throughout the Commonwealth, while there are no limitations on the number of tray submissions an SFA can submit, DESE will only be awarding one monthly Terrific Tray award per SFA in a given school year.

02

Additionally, to encourage participation by new districts and recognize new Terrific Trays, past monthly Terrific Tray winners who received a monetary prize are not eligible to win again for the next two Terrific Tray Competition cycles (the two fiscal years following their monetary award).

03

We still encourage past winners to submit entries during this period. Doing so allows them to stay engaged and be recognized through the Terrific Tray Alumni Network. More information about the Alumni Network will be shared with past winners during the 2026–2027 school year.

Winner Benefits

Terrific Tray Monthly winners will:

  • Receive $2,500 to reinvest in your School Nutrition Program from the Henry P. Kendall Foundation

  • Be featured in DESE FNP's Fueling the Commonwealth Newsletter

  • Be featured in DESE FNP's Monthly Town Hall

  • Be featured on social media from partners including Mass. Farm to School, DESE, The John C. Stalker Institute for Food and Nutrition, and the School Nutrition Association of Massachusetts (Please Note: This list is not exhaustive.).

  • Receive local food swag from Massachusetts Farm to School

  • Compete for the title of Terrific Tray of the Year (the Terrific Tray of the Year winner will receive two free tickets to the upcoming Healthy Kids, Healthy Programs Summit for attendance at the Terrific Tray Award Ceremony)

Scoring

Terrific Tray scoring is conducted by a team of School Nutrition representatives including individuals from DESE's Office for Food and Nutrition Programs, Massachusetts Farm to School, as well as partner organizations.

When a Terrific Tray submission is received, the submission is eligible for a monthly Terrific Tray award at any point in the given school year (not just the month following the photograph's submission). The Terrific Tray review team utilizes the following scorecard guidance in the Terrific Tray selection process:

01

Photo Quality

Resolution, clarity and composition

02

Tray Composition

All components of a reimbursable meal are incorporated, serving sizes are appropriate, unflavored milk or a nutritionally equivalent milk alternative is represented and the tray avoids use of plastic wrappers and disposables

03

Geographic Diversity

Submission is from a district that has not won at Terrific Tray, as well as submissions from currently underrepresented regions including the Berkshires, South Shore, Lower and Outer Cape/Islands, and Central Massachusetts

04

Recipe Readability and Creativity

Recipes featured on the tray incorporate scratch cooked elements, are culturally-responsive, have been influenced by student engagement in the menu planning process and are based on a submitted standardized recipe(s) designed for institutional production

05

Local Ingredients

At least 2-3 Massachusetts grown/produced items are represented, potentially alongside other regionally produced items, and the tray represents a novel procurement relationship such as a direct purchase with a farmer, the intentional alignment with the Massachusetts Harvest of the Month Program, the sourcing of a meat/meat alternate including poultry, beef, seafood, beans, or dairy, or the sourcing of a unique item such as grain, a non-traditional fruit or vegetable, or a novel minimally processed item that incorporates local ingredients

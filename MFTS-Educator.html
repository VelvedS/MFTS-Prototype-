Programs for Educators at Every Level

Whether you teach kindergartners or high schoolers, tend a school garden or focus on social studies, there's a MA Farm to School program designed to fit your classroom and curriculum.

Harvest of the Month

Harvest of the Month

Designed for classrooms, this program introduces students to a new locally grown, raised, or caught crop each month. Upon registration, teachers plus a monthly email with Massachusetts standards-aligned curriculum, resources, book recommendations, and more.

Learn more
Year-long Cohort

Farm to School Institute

A year-long professional learning opportunity for school and early education teams. Participating teams develop a school-wide Farm to School Action Plan, receive one-on-one coaching from experienced farm to school practitioners, and connect with peers across the state.

Learn more
Grades 7-12

Food System Civic Engagement Project

An initiative empowering secondary students and educators to become changemakers in their local food systems. Teams take a deep dive into how food connects to civic engagement, sustainability, and social justice, then design and lead a real community project.

Learn more

Free Workshops, all year long

MA Farm to School offers free virtual professional development throughout the school year for classroom educators, garden educators, school nutrition staff, and advocates. Most sessions are recorded and archived.

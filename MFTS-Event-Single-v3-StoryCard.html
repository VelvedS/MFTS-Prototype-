Harvest of the Month

October Apples Celebration.

Saturday, October 18·10:00 AM·MFTS Farm at Hampshire County

Every October, we open the orchard. Families, classrooms, farmers, and food service directors come together for a morning that puts October's Harvest of the Month, the Massachusetts apple, on the tray, in the lesson plan, and in the hand.

The morning begins with a guided walk through the orchard at peak picking, followed by a tasting flight of nine Massachusetts apple varieties, side by side. The kids run the cider press; the adults pick up the classroom recipe cards that go home with every school in the program for the rest of the month.

It is the loosest, friendliest event on our calendar. Bring boots, bring kids, bring an empty bag for apples to take home.

Program for the morning

  • 10:00 AMWelcome at the farm stand. Coffee from Atlas Coffee in Easthampton, cider donuts from the farm bakery.
  • 10:30 AMOrchard walk with farm staff. Bring boots; the lower rows can be muddy.
  • 11:30 AMTasting flight: nine Massachusetts apple varieties, sweetest to most acidic. Side by side, with notes.
  • 12:15 PMKid-built cider press. Adults can watch and clap.
  • 1:00 PMRecipe-card pickup and farm-stand u-pick. Stay as long as you like.

What to bring

Boots, a bag for apples, layers, and a thermos. We supply tasting plates, cider press parts, and recipe cards. Classroom teachers: bring a list of how many recipe-card sets you need for your school, and we'll bundle them at pickup.

The event is fully outdoors and runs rain or shine. In the case of severe weather, the tasting and recipe pickup move into the farm stand.

MA FRESH Grant

Funding Your Farm to School Program

The Massachusetts FRESH (Farming Reinforces Education and Student Health) Grant helps K–12 schools and early education programs start or grow local food purchasing, food literacy education, and farm partnerships.

On recordFY23 to FY26
Grants made113
Administered byDESE

What Does the Grant Program Fund

Eligibility and scope

MA FRESH is a statewide, competitive grant administered by the Massachusetts Department of Elementary and Secondary Education (DESE). It supports schools running the National School Lunch Program (NSLP) and licensed early education and care programs running the Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP) that want to build up local food purchasing and food system education whether they're just getting started or expanding an established program.

Impact of the Grant Program

Since 2023

Since launching in 2023, the MA FRESH grant has been driving local food sourcing and education across the Commonwealth.

Harvest of the Month

Promotional Materials

For registered Harvest of the Month participants — School Nutrition

This page features digital & print-ready resources available only for registered Harvest of the Month participants. These resources are intended to help you promote your program to students, staff and families.

We encourage you to use these materials widely and ask that you do not alter the graphics except in the cases where we have explicitly provided a template for you to adapt, such as the menu template. Please contact Dena at dena@massfarmtoschool.org with any questions about the materials.
